## Enduring decisions

- Full coordinate-owned PCG64DXSM streams replace scalar seed fingerprints.
  Worker count, scheduling and recovery do not own randomness.
- Safety-limit attempts have no winner/rank. Tournament primary performance
  counts all attempted player exposures; completed distributions, TrueSkill
  and H2H score tests keep their explicit completed-game conditioning.
- Artifact contract v3 binds scopes, schemas, source/method identity and
  completion. Old bytes cannot be promoted by replacing sidecars. Process-local
  authenticated snapshots never replace the fresh final root/root/pair audit.
- Roots and player counts run sequentially. Independent admitted stage units
  use bounded scheduling and exact durable-unit recovery. A fixed 1 GiB
  process-tree ceiling is not a scientific or artifact-validity invariant.
- Simulation owns publication; ingest coalesces adjacent batches; combine
  retains by-k bytes and an ordered concat manifest. Optional local scratch
  uses bounded cross-volume publication without moving canonical checkpoints.
- Two-root stability is fixed-design descriptive reproducibility. Screening
  and prediction do not establish a unique best strategy or causal effects.
- H2H plans are immutable; exact-plan cap approval/progress are separate.
  Statistical advantage, practical dominance and pairwise equivalence are
  independent conclusions. Equivalence is not transitive.

## Remediation experiments

Final JSON under `docs/remediation/` and tested benchmark drivers preserve
measurements, frozen gate parameters and conditional capacity models.

- Tasks 3A/3B and 4A-4D measured storage, RNG routing, H2H execution, graph
  authentication and recovery. The historical 6.3% storage-location effect
  missed its 10% scratch trigger. Local scratch was later added explicitly
  without changing statistical methods.
- Task 5A projected 10.47 days central and 19.73 days conservative before
  optimization. These were engineering projections, not observed production.
- Task 6 reduced exact route-local counts and compiled exact hashing/lag
  kernels. Bounded comparisons retained digests and measured 5.06x-9.06x
  speedups. A 64-partition alternative was 24.5% slower; production retained
  32. The August 27 rerun superseded earlier capacity coefficients.
- Task 7A separated fragmented historical seeds 54/55 from accepted seeds
  56/57. Its model was 127.30 hours central, 240.44 hours planning upper,
  `not_capacity_ready`. Both trees remain: tests read 54/55 and the density
  driver compares them with 56/57.
- Task 7B's width-16 H2H bundles preserved logical/downstream digests, but the
  primary publication improvement was 66.64% against a required 70%; simulation
  also missed its regression gate. The candidate was rolled back. Production
  remains per block. Final baseline/candidate repetitions and verdicts remain;
  failed development variants and quick/topology scratch are disposable.

## Remaining limits

Qualification does not establish production capacity. Sparse conditional
precision may be withheld; monitors do not certify independence; equivalence
power differs from superiority detection power. Broad opponent-composition
experiments, fixed-matchup winner entropy, state-level causal explanations,
prescribed rankings and an automatic figure suite remain outside this release.
Recheck live disk capacity before execution; historical observations reserve
no space.
